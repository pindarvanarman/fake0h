Stick Fight

Stand back so the camera can see you. Your stick figure copies everything you do, so fight for real!

Punch: punch fast toward the right side of the screen
Kick: kick a foot up high
Helicopter kick: spin all the way around, or hold your arms out wide like helicopter blades
Block: cross both arms in front of your face
Jump: jump! Jumping dodges punches and kicks
There may be a few secret moves, too…

Each fighter has 3 hearts. Knock out your opponent to win!
