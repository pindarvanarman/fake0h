Bloom & Grow
============

A peaceful gardening game. Nothing ever wilts, there are no timers, and your farm
saves by itself (plants keep growing while you're away).

How to play
- Pick a seed at the bottom, then click soil to plant it. Drag across the soil to
  plant lots at once.
- Click a growing plant to water it (from level 2): it grows twice as fast.
- Click ripe plants (they bounce and sparkle) to pick them. Drag to pick lots.
- Sell your basket with the orange button or by clicking the market stall.
- Selling gives coins and XP. Level up to grow your farm!
- Scroll or use the + and - buttons to zoom. Drag the grass to look around.
- Keys 1-9 pick seeds quickly.

The 16 levels
 1 Tiny Patch          carrots
 2 Garden Bed          lettuce, watering can
 3 Backyard Garden     strawberries, scarecrow
 4 Flower Corner       sunflowers, bench
 5 Little Farm         tomatoes, tool shed (Plant all)
 6 Pumpkin Patch       pumpkins, well (Water all)
 7 Busy Bee Farm       eggplants, beehive (+10% prices)
 8 Country Farm        corn, windmill
 9 Tulip Fields        tulips, duck pond
10 Big Red Farm        watermelons, barn (Pick all)
11 Vineyard Estate     grapes, greenhouse (20% faster growing)
12 Rose Garden         roses, fountain (+10% XP), visitors
13 Tropical Orchard    pineapples, fruit trees (+1 coin a second)
14 Flower Gardens      hibiscus, flower arch
15 Grand Conservatory  cherry blossoms, glass conservatory
16 Botanical Museum    prize bouquets, THE BOTANICAL MUSEUM (+5 coins a second)

Easy things to tweak (top of the script in index.html)
- SEEDS  : each crop's cost, selling price, growing time and unlock level
- LEVELS : each level's name, number of plots and what it unlocks
- XP_AT  : how much XP each level needs (change the 40 or the 1.62)

To start over, click ? and then "Start a new farm".
