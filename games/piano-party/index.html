Pindars Piano Party

Stand back so the camera can see your whole body. The floor is a giant piano: walk, stomp and jump to play notes with your feet.

Pop balloons with your hands to set off fireworks and drums. The more you move, the faster the beat and the more balloons fly by.

Pop 100 balloons to win. Watch out for bombs: if one goes off, bang the drums fast to get back up. Three bombs and it’s game over.
