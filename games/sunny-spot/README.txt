Sunny Spot
==========

You're a cat. Upstairs there's a comfy chair in a patch of sunshine, and it's
yours. Get there before the human does!

How to play
- Click where you want to go (or use WASD / arrow keys). Click your food bowl,
  a cat bed, or the human and the cat walks over and does the right thing.
- M: meow. Meow at your food bowl in the kitchen and the human drops what they're
  doing to come fill it, even if they're upstairs. Great for luring them away!
- P: purr next to the human to get petted (recharges you and keeps them busy).
- E or right click: eat from a full bowl, or nap in a cat bed.

Energy: the more you have, the faster you run. You need 50 energy to climb the
stairs, and you start sleepy. Balls of yarn and lizards are impossible to resist:
touch yarn and you have to play with it, get near a lizard and you have to chase
it. Both use up energy. After a distraction, the cat keeps going to where you clicked.

The human's to-do list is shown at the top. When they finish it, they head for
the chair. If they sit in it first, you lose.

Easy things to tweak (top of the script in index.html)
- START_ENERGY, STAIRS_ENERGY   : how sleepy you start, energy needed for stairs
- CAT_SLOW, CAT_FAST            : cat speed with no / full energy
- YARN_COST, LIZARD_COST        : how tiring the distractions are
- FOOD_GAIN, NAP_GAIN, PET_GAIN : how much each kind of recharge gives
- HUMAN_SPEED and ROUTE         : how fast the human is and their to-do list
